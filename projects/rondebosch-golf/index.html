RONDEBOSCH GOLF

Architectural and interior design proposal for the Rondebosch Golf Club, integrating modern amenities with classic club aesthetics.

Client Rondebosch Golf Club
Type Hospitality / Commercial
Phase Proposal · 2024
Key Scope Concept Design
SCROLL TO EXPLORE
The design proposal for Rondebosch Golf Club refines the classic club model with a sequence of modernized lounges, dining rooms, and spectator terraces. Focused on clean detailing and contextual materiality, the architectural intervention repositions the clubhouse to engage directly with the golf course landscapes.
