Proof Module 01

Massing Strategy & Design Intent

Hover to explore ↔

Axonometric studies established the massing language and phased delivery strategy across the development.

Before documentation began, the spatial relationships between retail units, parking zones, and public interfaces were tested at massing level. The axonometric views provided the client with a clear understanding of the bulk and break-down of the scheme prior to detailed Revit modelling.

Proof Module 02

Shop 17 — Council Submission

A standalone council submission for Shop 17, coordinated within the broader Berkshire Boulevard framework.

Shop 17 required its own discrete submission package — independently coordinated but fully integrated into the overarching council drawing set. All drawings were produced in Revit and formatted to meet Western Cape council standards.

Proof Module 03

Council Drawing Set — Berkshire Boulevard

Full council submission drawing set across the Berkshire Boulevard retail development.

The council submission comprised site plans, floor plans, sections, elevations, and schedules — all produced in Revit and coordinated across the multiple retail units. Revision management tracked changes across stakeholder review cycles up to final submission. Full coordination plans with all consultants.
