STUD HQ

Client Stud HQ
Type Interior Design Rollout
Phase Rollout · Multi-Site
Key Scope 3D Visualisation + Interior Design
SCROLL TO EXPLORE
As Designer and 3D Artist on the Stud HQ rollout, my scope covered the development and production of interior design proposals and 3D visualisations across multiple South African locations. Each site required adapting the brand's spatial language to unique floor plans and tenancy conditions — maintaining visual consistency while accommodating site-specific constraints.
Designer 3D Artist Interior Rollout Multi-Site Visualisation
Proof Module 01

Bloemfontein — Flagship Proposal

Interior design proposal for the Bloemfontein flagship — establishing the visual language for the national rollout.

The Bloemfontein location served as the visual benchmark for the brand rollout. The design concept focused on a strong identity wall, curated material palette, and strategic lighting to create a premium barbershop environment that would translate consistently across all subsequent sites.

Proof Module 02

Hazelwood Pretoria — Spatial Adaptations

Developing the branding and fit-out language for the Hazelwood Pretoria location.

Hazelwood adapted the brand's primary spatial components to a Pretoria context. Renders tested light penetration, floor finish thresholds, and custom signage details to ensure site delivery aligned with the client's premium visual standard.

Proof Module 03

Kloof Street — Technical Coordination

Technical drawing set and finish coordination for the Kloof Street tenancy.

Kloof Street required detailed plan sets, elevation studies, and coordinated interior finishes. All spatial dimensions were resolved in response to the specific commercial shell conditions, delivering a clear blueprint for construction-readiness.

Proof Module 04

Sea Point — Equipment Layouts

Equipment layout, plumbing, and visual design adaptations for Sea Point.

The Sea Point branch presented a complex site layout. Deliverables included equipment placements, lighting coordinates, plumbing drops, and finish details. Dynamic digital screens were coordinated to elevate the customer experience inside the store.

Proof Module 05

Cavendish Proposals — Spatial Walkthrough

Virtual walkthroughs and conceptual spatial massing for the Cavendish site.

The Cavendish site required detailed dynamic walkthroughs to convey spatial massing, circulation routes, and customer experience. These videos present the sequence of interior experiences tailored specifically for this retail footprint.
