HOUSE PATHER

Residential renovation and extension for a private client in Bloubergstrand, completed in 2023. Architect: JAKUPA Architects and Urban Designers.

Client Private

Proof Module 01

Architectural Renders & Design Intent

Exterior renders communicating the design intent and materiality of the proposed renovation.

All renders were produced to communicate the final design to the client and relevant stakeholders. The visualisations focused on the relationship between the new addition and the existing structure, with particular attention to material contrast, landscaping, and the coastal context of the Bloubergstrand setting.

Proof Module 02

Construction Documentation

Architectural drawing set produced to support the planning and construction phases of the project.

The technical documentation package covered all required plans, sections, and elevations — drafted in accordance with JAKUPA's design intent and coordinated for submission and construction-readiness. Revision 1 of the drawing set incorporated client and planning feedback.

Proof Module 03

Interior & Detail Studies

Interior renders and detail studies developed to resolve spatial and material questions.

Interior visualisations were produced to allow the client to evaluate finish selections, spatial proportions, and the relationship between interior and exterior. These studies informed final specification decisions prior to construction commencement.
