Case Study · Luxury Residential

ZIMBALI ESTATE

Luxury residential design and construction documentation for a private residence within the Zimbali Estate — from architectural proposal through to construction-ready floor plans.

Client Private
Type Luxury Residential
Phase Proposal → Construction
Key Scope Architecture + Construction Documentation
Proof Module 01

Architectural Design Renders

Architectural renders establishing the design language and spatial quality of the proposed residence.

The design proposal focused on the relationship between the built form and the Zimbali Estate landscape context — with considered attention to arrival sequence, view orientation, and the integration of indoor-outdoor living into the spatial planning.

Proof Module 02

Construction Documentation

Construction-ready floor plans produced across all levels of the residence.

The documentation package covered ground, first, and third floor plans — all produced to construction standard and coordinated for contractor use. The floor plan set was developed in parallel with the architectural design, ensuring spatial decisions were directly reflected in the technical drawings.

Proof Module 03

Floor Plans — All Levels

Multi-level floor plans coordinated across the full residential programme.

The three-level residential programme required careful coordination between floor plates — managing structural grids, vertical circulation, and service zones across all levels. Each plan was produced to a consistent drawing standard and formatted for estate authority submission.
