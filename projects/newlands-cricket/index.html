Concept Renders & Spatial Studies

Hover to explore

Concept renders establishing the architectural character and spatial quality of proposed interventions.

The concept phase tested a range of architectural approaches for various intervention areas across the ground. Render studies focused on spectator experience, arrival sequence, and the relationship between new interventions and the existing stadium fabric.

Proof Module 02

Proposal Documentation

Architectural drawings produced across multiple proposal phases, coordinating design intent with technical requirements.

Proposal documentation was developed in response to ongoing client and stakeholder feedback. Drawing sets covered floor plans, sections, elevations, and key details — progressing through revision cycles to meet submission deadlines for each phase.

Proof Module 03

Site & Context Photography

Site documentation and context studies informing the architectural response to the existing ground.

Site photography and contextual analysis informed key design decisions — including sight-line management, materials selection, and the integration of new elements into the existing heritage character of Newlands Cricket Ground.
