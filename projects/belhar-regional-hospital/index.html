Case Study · BIM Coordination

BELHAR REGIONAL HOSPITAL

Operating as a Senior Architectural Technologist under 2K20 Architects, I managed the multi-block technical coordination, BIM alignment, and consultant layer integration for the Belhar Regional Hospital development.

Client Western Cape Government (WCG)
Sector Public Healthcare · 642-Bed Facility
Stage IDMS Stage 2 · Concept Design
Key Scope BIM Coordination & Clash Resolution

Proof Module 01

Hospital-Scale Context & Multi-Block Architecture

Directed comprehensive technical coordination across multiple blocks, integrating structural lift cores, escape stairs, public circulation, and docking yards with critical site and building entrance elevations.

By utilizing the multi-block level plans, my coordination workflow linked structural cores, vertical risers, and primary circulation pathways across blocks A through G. My specific contribution focused on ensuring that the horizontal circulation widths, firewall splits, and structural junctions were systematically updated and aligned across the BIM model as layout changes occurred.

Proof Module 02

Workshops Layout & Constraints Handling

Site boundaries and underground service yard constraints dictated a split-workshop layout that required extensive departmental planning.

Due to site yard limitations and a buried diesel storage tank, the workshops were split: the Maintenance Workshop was placed in Block B (Level 0) and the Clinical Engineering Workshop in Block C (Level 1). This separation created immediate challenges with circulation, staff facilities distribution, and dispatch splits.

Process Note: The layout progressed from Proposal 1 through multiple client review cycles to Rev 03. Feedback focused on corridor efficiency, natural light distribution, access control, and fire safety compliance in proximity to the diesel tank.
Proof Module 03

Level 4 Orthopaedics Coordination & 3D Clash Resolution

Resolving conflicts between heavy mechanical services and structural grids in the ceiling void of a typical hospital ward layout.

The orthopaedics package required coordinating technical interfaces in high-density areas. Using 2D coordination cuts (at 50mm, 1200mm and 3700mm above FFL) in tandem with Revit 3D clash views, I identified and documented coordination clashes between all consultants. Finding these conflicts in the virtual stage avoided costly redesigns and delays during the on-site build.

Proof Module 04

Circulation Logic & Multi-Block Connectivity

Hover to explore ↔

Linking structural cores and primary circulation pathways across blocks A through G.

The scale of Belhar Regional Hospital required a robust circulation logic to handle high-traffic patient transport, staff movement, and service flows. My contribution focused on mapping out and verifying these multi-block circulation strategies. By linking the structural cores with primary horizontal pathways, we ensured that the massive scale of the hospital remained functional, navigable, and compliant with critical fire safety and egress constraints.
