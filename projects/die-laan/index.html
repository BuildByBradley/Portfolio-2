Proof Module 01

Boundary Wall & Walkway Materiality

Proposed material upgrades to the boundary wall and pedestrian walkway, tested prior to purchase.

The wall and walkway were the primary decision points for the buyer. Alternative cladding and paving options were presented as rendered proposals, allowing a direct comparison between the existing condition and the proposed transformation.

Proof Module 02

Spatial Context & Site Relationship

Contextual study showing the spatial relationship between the proposed upgrades and the broader property.

The proposal was developed to show not only the material change but how it read within the site context — ensuring the proposed upgrades were proportionate and architecturally coherent with the existing structure.
